# 12.1 — A single runaway script keeps the tick time high when few players are online.

**Status:** ✅ · **Size:** 5 SP

**A single runaway script keeps the tick time high when few players are online.** Found with the load harness (`npm run load`, see [load testing](../../../testing/load-testing.md)): `--players 5 --duration 40` gives a tick p95 of about 115 ms and 13 % of the ticks overran (limits: 80 ms and 5 %); `--players 5 --hogs 0` is healthy (p95 about 11 ms, no overruns), and so is `--players 50` with 10 hogs (p95 about 10 ms, no overruns, the 10 hogs killed). In the 5-player run the one hog (`for (;;) {}`) is the missing execution (9 of 10 alive), so the engine does kill it, yet the tick time stays high for the whole run. Find the cause and fix it, or, if it is only an artifact of the harness with very few scripts, fix the harness and document it. Hypotheses, none confirmed: the cost of killing a hog and rebuilding its QuickJS sandbox (`dispose()` in `quickjs-script-runtime.ts`); a hog that is launched or cut again and again; `ENGINE_SLICE_MS` (5 ms) not honoured; the way the harness assigns hogs in tiny runs; the window of the `botrts_engine_tick_p95_milliseconds` gauge. Deliver a reproduction as an engine test with one runaway script (injected clock and RNG), the fix, the numbers in [load testing](../../../testing/load-testing.md) updated if they change, and a `CHANGELOG.md` line
