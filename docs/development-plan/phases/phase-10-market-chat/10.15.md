# 10.15 — Market analytics and monitoring in the console

**Status:** ⬜ · **Agent:** C2 · **Size:** 5 SP

**Market analytics and monitoring in the console** (design §25.3, Market and chat; added 2026-10-15 after a check found that 10.11 had the ledger, the money supply, the treasury, the trade-volume charts and the moderation actions, but no view of the market itself): **(a) The market, as an admin sees it:** an order-book page (every resource and script and blueprint market, the open orders by price with the depth, the spread) and an auctions page (open, ending soon, finished, bids, anti-snipe extensions), each readable by a row. **(b) Statistics and charts** (a window of 24 hours, 7 and 30 days, with CSV export that is audited like the exports of 9.11): price history per resource with the volume, the number of trades and of active listings, volume by resource and by market, the fees by kind that reached the treasury, the credits minted by admins, the top traders and the top sellers, the share of players who traded (the funnel of 9.11 gets the step "first trade"), and the chat volume and the reports by day. **(c) Price anomalies:** the flags that 10.10 raises (a price far outside its band, wash-trading and multi-account patterns) as a list with the players and trades behind it, a status (new, reviewed, dismissed, acted on), a link to the profile of 9.4 and to the actions of 10.11. **(d) Monitoring:** the market joins the metric catalog and the alert rules of 9.2: the open orders and the matching latency, the ledger reconciliation result of the job of 10.10 (a mismatch is **red**), the treasury balance (main and pending trades), the deliveries waiting for inbox space, the rate of refused orders, and the size of the queues of the inbox and the transfers, with a market area on the dashboard of 9.3 and the thresholds in the generated table of 9.15. Read-only: it adds no way to change a balance. Includes: the read models and their integration tests against PostgreSQL (with numbers that are checked against the ledger), the permissions (`market.read`, `market.analytics`; owners, admins and analysts), the `authorization-matrix` entries, a Cypress spec (a trade made in the game shows in the order book, the price history and the volume; a flagged trade appears in the list and is reviewed), and the pages of the admin documentation in English and Russian
