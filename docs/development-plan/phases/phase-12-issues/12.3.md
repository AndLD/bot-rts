# 12.3 — A new base cannot craft anything: its storage starts with oil only.

**Status:** ✅ · **Size:** 3 SP

**A new base cannot craft anything: its storage starts with oil only.** Found by the harness quality review of 2026-10-17 (finding H-20261017-15 in `docs/quality-reviews/harness/2026-10-17.md`, PR #209): the starter kit has no drill and its storage starts with 50 oil only (`api/src/modules/domain/module-catalog.ts`, design §6.1); every recipe of design §9.3 needs iron, crystal or gold, only a drill extracts resources, and the base cannot work on itself, so the tutorial step "craft a module, assemble a bot" (design §16.1) cannot be done with what the game gives. **Owner decision (2026-10-17):** the resources needed for a first craft, iron included, are deposited in the base storage automatically when the base is deployed. **Owner decision (2026-10-21) on the amounts:** no confirmation is needed before coding; the task chooses just enough of each resource for the basic crafting of the tutorial path (the first module to craft and the first bot to assemble, derived from `DEFAULT_RECIPES`) plus a small margin, and records the arithmetic in its log; the numbers are revisited during balancing. The amounts are balance configuration, not constants, and fit the starter storage capacity (200) or raise it explicitly. The deposit is part of the engine's deploy-base command (single writer, `AGENTS.md` §9). Deliver a test that fails first (a freshly deployed base can afford the first craft and the first assembly), the deposit at deployment, design §6.1 and §16.1 updated, and a changelog fragment in `docs/product-knowledge/changelog.d/`
